Encore

Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Encore has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It doesn't collect analytics or sell anything about you. Everything it shows is worked out on your Mac.

What goes online, and why

  • Lyrics are looked up on LRCLIB using the song's title, artist and length.
  • Album art is fetched from Apple's iTunes search, only when your music app doesn't provide it.
  • Weather comes from Open-Meteo, using your location rounded to about 1 km, or a town you type in. Your Mac's location is only used with your permission.
  • Music videos, if you turn them on, are found and played from YouTube. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to the video player.
  • The AI fill, if you choose to download it, is fetched once from Hugging Face (about 187 MB). It runs entirely on your Mac; your album covers are never uploaded.
  • Video timings: how far each music video is from its song is shared anonymously, so videos line up straight away for everyone. Only the video's ID and that number are sent.
  • Updates are checked on GitHub.
  • Your licence key is checked with Polar, which sells Encore. Polar handles your payment and email address under its privacy policy; Encore never sees your card details.

On your Mac

Encore listens to the sound playing on your Mac only to make its visualiser and music-video timing work. Nothing is recorded or saved. Calendar events and call details are shown on your Mac and never leave it.
