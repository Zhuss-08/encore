Questions

 

I already had Encore (or Lyrics Screensaver). Do I need to pay?

No. If you had it before it became paid, it stays free on that Mac, with updates. Thank you for being an early user!

What happens when the trial ends?

Encore asks you to buy it and pauses until you do. Your settings are kept, so everything is just as you left it once you enter your key.

Can I use my key on more than one Mac?

Yes, on your own Macs. If you replace a Mac, remove the licence in Encore's Settings → Licence to free up its place.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Encore doesn't work for you, ask within 14 days of buying for a full refund, using the link in your Polar receipt email.

Is it safe? What does it collect?

There are no accounts, ads or tracking. Encore only goes online for lyrics, album art, the weather (using your location rounded to about 1 km), music videos from YouTube if you turn them on, anonymous video timings, the optional AI fill download, updates, and checking your licence key. See the privacy policy.

What is the AI fill, and does it send my music anywhere?

It's optional. Some covers can't simply be widened, like a face filling the whole cover, so Encore can use a small AI model to paint in the sides. It's a one-time download of about 187 MB, and it runs entirely on your Mac: nothing is uploaded. Without it, those covers are shown zoomed instead.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac with macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. The Dynamic Island looks best on a MacBook with a notch.
